# Doctor4Africa Case Study Outreach

## Background

Doctor4Africa (and specifically the AWS Glue data platform Parthiban built) is one of the strongest proof points of real-world engineering impact that can be directly tied to Parthiban's work.

**This is a Tech Nation "Significant technical contribution" + "Public-interest impact" evidence.**

---

## Call Script (30 min target)

### Opening (2 min)
"Hi [Cofounder Name], thanks for taking the time. I'm documenting some of the work I've done over the years for a visa application process. I wanted to reach out because the AWS Glue pipeline we built together for Doctor4Africa is one of the most impactful projects I've worked on — and I'd love to tell that story properly."

### Problem Statement (3 min)
"From what I remember, you were dealing with a significant data preparation bottleneck. Healthcare provider records needed extraction, validation, and verification before they could be processed. Manual data entry was slowing you down. Walk me through what the situation was like before we solved it?"

**Listen for:**
- Volume of records
- Manual effort required
- Time spent on data prep
- Impact on your operations

### Solution Narrative (3 min)
"What we built was an AWS Glue-based pipeline that automated the extraction and verification workflow. The key insight was that you didn't just need to extract data — you needed to verify it was correct before it reached your downstream systems."

**Key points to establish:**
- What the system does
- Why the approach matters
- How it reduced manual work

### Metrics & Impact (5 min)
"Can you help me quantify the impact? I'd love to understand:
- How many healthcare provider records has the system processed?
- What was the time investment per record before, and what is it now?
- Has this freed up your team to focus on higher-value work?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Total records processed (X,000+)
- Time savings per record (Y minutes → Z minutes)
- Percentage improvement (manual time saved)
- Team impact (freed capacity for other work)

### Verification & Next Steps (2 min)
"This is really valuable for documenting the work. Would you be comfortable if I mentioned Doctor4Africa by name in a case study? And could I ask for a short quote about how this has helped your operations?"

**Ideal quote formats:**
1. "Doctor4Africa processed [X] healthcare provider records using Parthiban's AWS Glue platform, reducing manual data preparation time by [Y]%."
2. "The automated verification workflow freed our team from data prep bottlenecks, allowing us to focus on patient outcomes."
3. "[Platform] gave us confidence that incoming data was verified before reaching critical systems."

### Closing (1 min)
"Thank you so much. This helps me show real impact for the visa application, and it's a story I'm proud to tell."

## What NOT to Say

❌ "Can you give me a testimonial to boost my visa application?"  
✅ "I'd like to document the real impact we built together."

❌ "I need proof of impact from you."  
✅ "Can you help me quantify the business outcome?"

❌ "How much time/money did we save?"  
✅ "Can you walk me through the metrics: records processed, time per record, team impact?"
